Privacy policy

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: contact details, load details and, for drivers, your experience.

How we use it

To answer your request, quote and move your freight, and review driver applications. We may call, text or email you about that request.

Who sees it

Our team and the service providers that help us run the business. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

We set no tracking cookies. Fonts and photos load from Google and Unsplash, which receive your IP address as part of normal web traffic.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, email dispatch@nodo.example.
